This project is a poetic inquiry into the deconstruction of place and an exploration of its future ruins.
The investigation did not follow a linear trajectory. Instead, it unfolded through continuous shifts of
perspective between the fragment and the whole, and from the whole back to the fragment.
Why an island?
The island becomes a point of departure for an investigation into place, time, memory and transformation.

The island is understood not as an isolated entity, but as a condition of separation and rebeginning.

Therefore, the island.
